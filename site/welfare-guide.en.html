Finding welfare support in Japan

Official places in Japan where you can ask for help with money, housing, work, disability, care, family or children. If you are not sure where to start, read "Where should I go first?". Most consultations are free. Many of the linked pages are in Japanese only.

Where should I go first?

  • If in doubt, go to your city, town or village office and tell the welfare section (such as the welfare office) that you need help. They will direct you to the right service.
  • If you are struggling with living costs, local "self-reliance support" centres help with work, housing and household finances together (MHLW: support system for people in need, Japanese).
  • See also the nationwide services by topic and the prefecture pages below.

In an emergency or when things are hard

  • If a life is in danger, call 119 (ambulance/fire) or 110 (police) without hesitation.
  • If you think a child may be abused: child guidance centre hotline "189" (Children and Families Agency, Japanese).
  • If you are struggling emotionally: the MHLW site "Mamorou yo Kokoro" (Japanese) lists phone and social media support services.
