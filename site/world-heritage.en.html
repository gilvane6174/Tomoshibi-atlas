What is World Heritage?

World Heritage properties are places listed on the World Heritage List under the Convention concerning the Protection of the World Cultural and Natural Heritage, adopted by the UNESCO General Conference in 1972, as having “outstanding universal value” for humanity. There are three kinds: cultural, natural and mixed. The Convention entered into force for Japan in 1992.

As of August 2026 there were 1,273 properties in total (991 cultural, 240 natural and 42 mixed), and 196 States Parties to the Convention, according to the Agency for Cultural Affairs of Japan (including the results of the 48th session of the World Heritage Committee in July 2026).
